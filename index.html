Frequently asked

Good questions, real answers

Are your cleaners insured?

Yes. Every Mamita Mary team member is fully insured and bonded. We carry $2M liability so you and your home are covered.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Always. We bring premium professional-grade supplies and equipment. Eco-friendly products are available on request at no extra cost.

What if I'm not satisfied?

Our satisfaction guarantee: if anything isn't right, tell us within 24 hours and we come back to fix it free of charge.

Do I need to be home?

Not at all. Many of our clients give us a key or door code. Our team is fully vetted and trained for unsupervised cleans.

How do you price?

Flat-rate pricing based on home size and service type. We send you a transparent quote within the hour — no hidden fees, no surprises.

Can I cancel or reschedule?

Yes. Just give us 24 hours notice and there's no fee. Same-day cancellations may incur a 50% charge.
